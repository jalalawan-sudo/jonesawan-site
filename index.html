Energy & climate policy analyst · Los Angeles, California

I launched Jones-Awan as a volunteer practice to take on projects that are analytically interesting, have real potential for policy change, and deepen my connections in the California regulatory world.

Most recently, I joined the Venice Neighborhood Council Land Use and Planning Committee to bring that same orientation to local community issues in Los Angeles.

Making the rules legible
